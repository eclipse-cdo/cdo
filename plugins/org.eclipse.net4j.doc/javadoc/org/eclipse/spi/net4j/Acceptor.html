public abstract class Acceptor extends org.eclipse.net4j.util.container.Container<IConnector> implements InternalAcceptor
Manages a listening Net4j transport endpoint and the connectors accepted through it. The acceptor applies transport configuration and negotiation settings to incoming connections.

      public void addConnector(InternalConnector connector)
      Adds an active connector or one undergoing deferred activation to this acceptor and publishes its addition. A connector that terminates during registration is removed again after its lifecycle listener has been installed, so a missed early lifecycle event cannot leave stale accepted state.
      Parameters:
      connector - the connector to track
