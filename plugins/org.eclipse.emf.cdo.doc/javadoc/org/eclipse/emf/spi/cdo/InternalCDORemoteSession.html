Interface InternalCDORemoteSession

All Superinterfaces:
CDORemoteSession, Comparable<CDORemoteSession>
public interface InternalCDORemoteSession extends CDORemoteSession
Internal representation of a client session connected to the local session through remote-session notifications. Its subscription state is maintained by the remote-session manager as topics are subscribed to or left.
